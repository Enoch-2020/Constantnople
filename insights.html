Our Technology

The Mobile App

The Constantnople mobile app puts the power of IoT monitoring in the hands of every fish farmer — making real-time intelligence simple, accessible, and actionable.

1

History

The mobile app was born from a real challenge — farmers in Kenya struggled with managing fish farms remotely. Without a way to monitor water conditions from a distance, they risked arriving too late to prevent fish losses. Constantnople set out to solve this by creating a simple, affordable tool that keeps farmers connected to their farms at all times.

2

Core Message & Purpose

To make fish farming smart, simple, and sustainable for every farmer in Africa. The app is designed to be the bridge between traditional farming knowledge and modern technology — not replacing the farmer, but empowering them with the information they need to make better decisions every day.

3

How the App Makes Farmers' Lives Easier

The app is built with simplicity and ease of use as a priority. Farmers can:

  • Monitor water quality (oxygen, temperature, pH) in real time
  • Receive instant alerts when conditions become unfavorable
  • Access simple dashboards with clear insights — no technical expertise required
  • Make faster decisions without being physically present at the farm

This reduces stress, saves time, minimizes losses, and improves overall farm management.

4

First Customer Experience

The first person to use the app was a fish farmer in Kenya's Rift Valley region. They were struggling with high fish mortality rates caused by sudden drops in oxygen levels overnight. After installing the IoT sensors and using the app, they received their first alert in the middle of the night. They were able to take immediate action — and that batch of fish was saved. That moment proved the real-world value of the technology and became the foundation for everything that followed.

5

First Prototype

The first version of the app was a minimal but functional product built to validate the core concept. It included:

  • Basic water quality monitoring (pH, temperature, turbidity, dissolved oxygen)
  • Simple alert notifications via SMS and app
  • Basic data visualization charts
  • Remote access via mobile device
6

Improvements & Current Version

Since the first prototype, the app has been significantly upgraded based on real farmer feedback and field testing. The current version features:

  • More advanced analytics and trend analysis
  • Improved UI/UX for better usability across devices
  • Integration with additional sensor types
  • More reliable and near-real-time data streaming
  • Enhanced alert system with customizable thresholds
  • Multi-cage and multi-farm management from one dashboard
7

Future Roadmap

Looking ahead, Constantnople plans to expand the app's capabilities to serve more farmers and unlock greater value from the platform:

  • Advanced AI for predictive feeding and disease detection
  • Farm performance analytics and benchmarking
  • Integration with financial services (credit, insurance)
  • Offline functionality for low-connectivity areas
  • Expansion to support more users across regions
